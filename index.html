English ภาษาไทย (Thai)
Quick Summary: สนุกเขียน (Sanuk Khian) is an educational handwriting tracing game for children (Thai consonants ก–ฮ, English letters A–Z, and numbers 0–10). We do not collect personal data, require user accounts, or allow personalized ad tracking. The app displays family-safe, non-personalized advertisements provided by Google AdMob configured strictly for children (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy compliant, without Advertising ID).

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how สนุกเขียน (Sanuk Khian) handles information when you or your child use our mobile application. Protecting the privacy of children and families is our top priority.

2. Personal Information We Do Not Collect

สนุกเขียน (Sanuk Khian) does not ask for, collect, or store any personal user information on developer-operated servers, including:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user accounts.
  • Google Advertising ID (AD_ID is explicitly removed and disabled in the app).
  • Precise or approximate GPS location data.
  • Photos, videos, audio recordings, contacts, or files from your device.

3. Local On-Device Storage

To provide a smooth learning experience, the app stores only non-personal learning preferences locally on your device using Android DataStore Preferences:

  • Which characters/numbers have been completed (progress tracking).
  • Sound enable/mute preference.
  • Selected tracing brush color and unlocked special brush colors.

This information stays strictly on your device and is never transmitted to us. If Android system Auto Backup is enabled in your device settings, the Android operating system may include app preferences in your private OS backup.

4. Permissions & Text-to-Speech (TTS)

The app uses only standard install-time network permissions (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) required to load family-safe advertisements, and does not request any dangerous or sensitive runtime permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, or Storage). The Google Advertising ID permission (AD_ID) is explicitly removed.

Voice pronunciation of Thai and English characters uses your device's built-in Android Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine locally on the device. No voice audio is recorded by the app.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob — Child-Directed Non-Personalized Mode)

To keep core educational content (ก–ฮ, A–Z, and 0–10) free, the app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To protect children's privacy under COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play's Families Policy:

  • Child-Directed & Non-Personalized Only: Ad requests are tagged with AgeRestrictedTreatment.CHILD and restricted to maximum content rating G (General Audiences). Ads are contextual/non-personalized only—no behavioral profiling or interest-based retargeting is permitted.
  • No Advertising ID: The Android Advertising ID permission is excluded from the app manifest.
  • Supported Ad Placements: Separated banner ads outside interactive drawing areas, frequency-capped interstitial ads between completed lessons, and strictly opt-in rewarded ads for unlocking optional brush colors.
  • Third-Party Partner Policy: Google may process limited, non-personal technical data (such as IP address or coarse network request signals) strictly as needed to deliver and prevent fraud on non-personalized ads. Learn more at Google Advertising Policies.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

สนุกเขียน (Sanuk Khian) is designed for young learners and families. In full compliance with COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play's Families Policy, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), use only Google Play Families self-certified ad SDKs in child-directed mode, and include no user accounts, social login, or external social chat.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not maintain developer-side user accounts or collect personal profiles, there is no server-side account data to request removal for. Parents or users can permanently delete all locally saved progress and unlocked brush preferences at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings > Apps > สนุกเขียน > Storage > Clear data or by uninstalling the app.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if app features change in the future. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
